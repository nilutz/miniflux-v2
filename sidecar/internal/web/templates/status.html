Passages per entry is the ratio that makes disk growth predictable: the design spec assumed 4–6 and measured 13, which put its sizing estimate off by roughly 5x. Passage and entry counts are estimates (Postgres' own planner statistics, refreshed by ANALYZE/autovacuum), not an exact count — cheap to read regardless of corpus size, at the cost of lagging a burst of inserts briefly.
